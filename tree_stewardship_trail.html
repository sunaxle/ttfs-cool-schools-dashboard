Tree Stewardship

Connected lessons:

Tree Journaling, Tree Measurement, Nature Journaling, Tree Detectives, Trees and Parasitism

Connected videos:

Tree Height, Tree Measurement (Tree Age), Nature Journaling, Tree Care, Mindfulness in Nature, How Leaves help us Identify Plant Species, Plant ID with iNat

Objective:

Students learn to observe, monitor, and report on the health of trees over time, developing ongoing stewardship skills and ecological awareness.

Overview:

Studying a tree over time will allow students to observe seasonal changes and growth patterns from a specific tree species. In this activity, students/classes choose a tree (or a few trees) and spend the year recording observations and researching information. Students/classes will record weekly observations to build a record that can be compared back to the baseline data. The tracking sheet will be sent to UTRGV to help organize the data.

Materials:

Measuring tape (for DBH), Class observation log/journal, Pencil/clipboard, Optional: soil measuring probe

Method Step 1 - Observations (within first two weeks of school):

  • Identification: classes use school tree planting plan or iNaturalist. Optional: choose a nickname.
  • Tree Measurements: Diameter (DBH) at breast height. Optional: hug tree or string technique. Height via Tree Height Video technique.
  • Tree Health: happy/medium/sad checkpoint. Leaf color (bright green/dark green/brown). Branch leaf coverage.
  • Soil moisture: Test beneath canopy at critical root zone (drip line) using fingers or probe. Check mulch/leaf coverage.
  • Growth & wildlife: Note flowers, fruits, seeds, new leaves. Report unhealthy trees immediately.

Method Step 2 - Reporting:

Add observations to school bulletin board and UTRGV dashboard.
