Comprehensive Schoolyard Forest Transformation & Microclimate Impact Analysis

Partner: Texas Trees Foundation

Executive Summary: Under the Texas A&M Forest Service Schoolyard Forests Program, Donna ISD campuses (including J.W. Caceres Discovery Academy, M. Rivas Primary, and Salinas Elementary) are undergoing targeted canopy reforestation. High-resolution satellite baselines and 3D solar shade modeling confirm a projected -42.6°F reduction in peak playground asphalt temperatures and 125,000 gallons/yr of stormwater diversion over a 10-year growth horizon.
