Core Scientific Vocabulary (Bilingual Support / Glosario)

Microclimate (Microclima)
The climate of a small, specific area (like under a tree) that differs from the surrounding area.
Albedo (Albedo)
The fraction of solar energy reflected from a surface back into space (dark asphalt = low; light surfaces = high).
Transpiration (Transpiración)
The process where plants release water vapor through leaf stomata, cooling the surrounding air.
Biomass (Biomasa)
The total mass of living organisms (wood, leaves, roots) that stores carbon captured from the air.
