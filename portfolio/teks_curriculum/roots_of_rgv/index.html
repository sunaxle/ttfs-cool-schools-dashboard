Guided Lesson Plan (45 Min)

  1. Warm-Up (5m): Have students sit under the trees and get their hands dirty! Ask them to consider how people survived without modern stores.
  2. Core Activity (30m): Discuss the history of indigenous tribes in the Rio Grande Valley. Have students explore the interactive Mesquite tree to uncover its 4 traditional uses.
  3. Cool-Down (10m): Gather and discuss which indigenous use of the tree surprised them the most.

TEKS Pedagogy

Social Studies TEKS 113.15.b.2.A

Understand how Native American groups lived in Texas and adapted to the environment.

Why this matters:

Learning indigenous uses of native plants contextualizes history and demonstrates early sustainable practices.
