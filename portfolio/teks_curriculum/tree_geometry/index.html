Guided Lesson Plan (45 Min)

  1. Warm-Up (5m): Have students sit under the trees and get their hands dirty! Discuss how we can measure things without a tape measure (non-standard measurement).
  2. Core Activity (30m): Students measure their pacing steps or arm spans. They use these body measurements to pace out the width of the tree's shadow and calculate the canopy area in the app.
  3. Cool-Down (10m): Gather the class. Compare who found the largest canopy area and discuss why tree shade is important.

TEKS Pedagogy

Math TEKS 111.6.b.8.C

Model area formulas for parallelograms, trapezoids, and circles.

Why this matters:

Connecting abstract math formulas to physical, living objects (like trees) increases retention and engagement.
