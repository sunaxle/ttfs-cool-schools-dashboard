Guided Lesson Plan (45 Min)

  1. Warm-Up (5m): Have students sit under the trees, get their hands dirty, and take deep breaths. Hand out physical hardbound journals.
  2. Core Activity (30m): Students use the Prompt Generator in the app to get a sensory prompt, then draw or write their response in their PHYSICAL journal.
  3. Cool-Down (10m): Gather and have students share their favorite drawing or sentence from their journal.

TEKS Pedagogy

ELA TEKS 110.6.b.11.A

Compose literary texts, including personal narratives and poetry.

Why this matters:

Combining sensory observation with descriptive writing builds vocabulary and fosters an emotional connection to nature.
