State-Aligned K-12 Outdoor Learning

TEKS-Aligned Environmental Curriculum

Engage students with hands-on, standards-aligned micro-applications that bridge classroom theory and outdoor field science. Each module pairs guided 45-minute lesson plans with tri-lingual support (English, Español, TexMex) and interactive tools designed around campus microforest ecosystems.

Science TEKS 🌿

Campus Botanist

TEKS 112.14.b.9.B Food Webs & Energy Flow

Students examine campus producers and consumers, then drag and drop native organisms into an interactive food web starting with solar energy to calculate ecosystem health.

Key Activity: 45-min hands-on exploration of local producers, consumers, and predators under campus trees.

Fine Arts TEKS 🎨

Eco-Canvas

TEKS 117.111.b.1.A Environmental Art & Patterns

Connects sensory observation of bark textures and leaf shapes to artistic expression, pattern matching games, and physical paper bark rubbings under campus trees.

Key Activity: Matching leaf shapes with bark textures and creating tactile crayon bark rubbings.

A dynamic prompt generator that inspires student reflection, descriptive vocabulary, and creative poetry in physical hardbound journals while resting in school shade.

Social Studies TEKS 🪵

Roots of the RGV

TEKS 113.15.b.2.A Indigenous Ethnobotany

Discover the cultural and ecological heritage of South Texas indigenous communities by exploring traditional uses of the Honey Mesquite tree for food, tools, medicine, and shade.

Key Activity: Interactive 4-part Mesquite tree discovery highlighting indigenous adaptation and sustainable foraging.

Math TEKS 📐

Tree Geometry

TEKS 111.6.b.8.C Area Modeling & Pacing

Apply real-world geometry by pacing out tree shadows and using student body measurements to calculate canopy ground coverage, cooling shade area, and environmental benefits.

Key Activity: Hands-on non-standard pacing steps and mathematical canopy area formulas.
