Guided Lesson Plan (45 Min)

  1. Warm-Up (5m): Have students sit under the trees and get their hands dirty! Feel the bark and look at the leaves.
  2. Core Activity (30m): Students use the app to match the leaf shapes they found to the correct bark textures they felt. Then, they do a physical bark rubbing on paper.
  3. Cool-Down (10m): Share the bark rubbings and discuss how different trees have different patterns.

TEKS Pedagogy

Art TEKS 117.111.b.1.A

Explore ideas from life experiences about family, peers, and the natural environment.

Why this matters:

Observing patterns and textures in nature (like leaves and bark) inspires artistic expression and attention to detail.
