Guided Lesson Plan (45 Min)

  1. Warm-Up (5m): Have students sit under the trees and get their hands dirty! Discuss what bugs, birds, and plants they see right now.
  2. Core Activity (30m): Students search the campus for examples of producers and consumers. Then, they use the app to drag and drop these organisms into a balanced food web.
  3. Cool-Down (10m): Gather and ask students what would happen to the food web if the trees were removed.

TEKS Pedagogy

Science TEKS 112.14.b.9.B

Describe the flow of energy through food webs, beginning with the Sun.

Why this matters:

Understanding food webs helps students appreciate the interconnectedness of local ecosystems on their own campus.
