### 3. Summary of Deliverable Submissions Substantiating Invoice #4

1. **Deliverable A: Environmental Baseline Data Summary ($3,390.00 Earned):**
   - Standardized cartographic figure templates, uniform thermal/NDVI color ramps, and bilingual English/Spanish caption conventions across all 13 participating school sites (`docs/Modeling_Visuals_Package.md`).
   - Conducted regional 30% canopy baseline synthesis and allometric crown variance analysis evaluating the $1,314\text{ sq ft}$ planning constant against South Texas native species (*Quercus virginiana*, *Ulmus crassifolia*, *Sabal mexicana*) and quantifying the net +661 tree deficit across 8.45M sq ft of studied campus land (`docs/CANOPY_CONSTANT_1314_ANALYSIS.md`, `docs/REGIONAL_CANOPY_30_PERCENT_STUDY_MASTER.md`).
   - Integrated research lineage ledgers connecting district PEIMS attendance rates with NWS Brownsville extreme heat data, Park et al. cognitive regression curves, and TEA TEC §25.081 low-attendance waiver thresholds (`docs/research_provenance_and_lineage.md`).

2. **Deliverable C: Forecasting & Digital Dashboard Support Materials ($3,632.00 Earned):**
   - Implemented Dashboard v2 early component optimizations across 49 static pages: strict IIFE scoping, zero inline scripts/styles, external CDN preconnects, and native image lazy-loading.
   - Authored formal dashboard usability pass and data caveats documentation covering PEIMS attendance data granularity, diurnal surface temperature benchmarks (9 AM baseline 84°F, 1 PM peak 138°F asphalt vs 87°F shade, 5 PM retention 126°F), and Turf.js geometric validation tolerances (`docs/Dashboard_v2_Planning_Memo.md`).
   - Advanced interactive prototype modules: 3-tier Role-Based Access Control (`user_access_strategy.html`), zero-PII elementary Habitat Spotter (`biodiversity_analog_concept.html`), 10-year Living Tree Legacy cohort scrubber (`tree_diary.html`), and TEKS-aligned STEM curriculum portal (`teks_lesson_plans.html`).
