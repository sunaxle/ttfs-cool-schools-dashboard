These lesson plans are designed to integrate directly with the Field Measurements portal. Students collect anonymized data on campus and use it to complete TEKS-aligned scientific inquiry.

Curriculum & Lesson Plans

Grade 3 Science (TEKS 3.12.A)

Tree Temperature Diaries

Standard: Explain how temperature affects plant responses through dormancy.

Activity: Students use simple infrared (IR) thermometers to measure surface temperatures in three locations on campus: directly under a mature tree canopy, on a grassy field in the sun, and on asphalt/concrete.

Dashboard Integration: They log these temperatures into the Field Measurements portal along with observations of tree dormancy. Data is submitted anonymously as a team average.

Grade 6 Science (TEKS 6.12.A)

Habitat Spotting & Abiotic Mapping

Standard: Investigate how organisms depend on abiotic factors such as range of temperatures or soil composition.

Activity: Students map out their schoolyard to understand resource competition. They use soil moisture meters and ambient thermometers to collect data in different micro-habitats (e.g., near a large oak tree vs. near a portable building).

Dashboard Integration: Using the Field Measurements portal, students upload these measurements to create a simple, campus-wide heat and moisture map, keeping all data aggregated.

High School Environmental Systems (TEKS 112.50)

Campus Urban Heat Island (UHI) Audit

Standard: Evaluate the impact of human activities—such as urbanization—on natural systems.

Activity: Students conduct a rigorous audit comparing impervious surfaces (parking lots) to vegetated spaces (school gardens). They collect time-series temperature data over several days, measuring heat retention after the sun goes down.

Dashboard Integration: Students analyze the data to propose specific, data-backed locations for future McAllen ISD tree plantings. They can draft proposals based on where cooling interventions are most needed.
