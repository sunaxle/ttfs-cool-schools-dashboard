Why Claim a Tree?

Claiming a tree builds a personal connection between our campus community and the urban canopy. By giving a tree a name and a dedicated class or individual to look after it, we ensure these trees grow strong for generations of students.

  • 🌱 Connect: Learn about your tree's species and history.
  • 🌿 Steward: Report on its health and seasonal changes.
  • 🌳 Legacy: Leave your mark on the Cool Schools canopy.
