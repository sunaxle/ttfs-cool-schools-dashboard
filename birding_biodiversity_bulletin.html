Unified Curriculum: Birding, Biodiversity & Tree Health Stewardship

Active Campus: Donna ISD — M. Rivas Primary

Integrated Modules: Bioblitz Biodiversity Scout + Adopted Tree Weekly Health Monitoring

Standards: TEKS Science 3.9A, 4.9A, 5.9B (Ecosystems, Primary Producers & Biodiversity)

🎯 Unified Objective

Students observe their schoolyard forest as a living, interconnected ecosystem. Students monitor adopted campus trees (growth, DBH, canopy vigor, soil moisture) and track the birds, insects, and wildlife that depend on these trees for food, shelter, and nesting habitat.

📦 Field Materials

  • Tree Measurement Tapes (for DBH) & Clipboard field sheets
  • Birding & Biodiversity species identification guides
  • Optional: Soil moisture probe, magnifying glasses, binoculars

🔬 Field Protocol

Part 1: Adopted Tree Stewardship Check

  • Tree Nickname & Species: Locate your classroom's adopted campus tree.
  • Growth: Measure DBH trunk circumference at 4.5 ft height and estimate tree height.
  • Vigor & Health: Rate canopy condition (😊 Happy & Lush / 😐 Medium / 😟 Stressed / Dry). Check leaves for discoloration or insect damage.
  • Root Zone Moisture: Test soil at the canopy drip-line. Ensure mulch is properly spread without touching the trunk root flare.

Part 2: Birding & Biodiversity BioBlitz

  • Avian Census: Identify RGV signature birds (Chachalaca, Kiskadee, Green Jay, Woodpecker) perched, singing, or foraging.
  • Insect & Habitat Check: Count pollinators, beetles, and caterpillars supported by the tree.
  • Data Entry: Record counts directly onto the digital campus ledger.
