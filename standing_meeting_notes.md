## Program Goals
*   Increase the amount of time students spend outdoors.
*   Increase environmental stewardship behaviors among students.
*   Support outdoor learning and nature-based education.
*   Elevate the role and importance of campus maintenance teams.

## Desired Outcome
*   Develop a dashboard that measures and communicates project impact, including:
    *   Student time spent outdoors.
    *   Student participation in stewardship activities.
    *   Analysis of lesson plans that include outdoor learning components.
    *   Forecast and modeling comparison of ambient and surface temperatures, that estimate potential increase in outdoor learning days resulting from tree canopy interventions.
    *   Maintenance requirements and forecasting including estimated cost.
    *   Long-term stewardship and campus management metrics.
*   **Reference projects:**
    *   https://www.greenschoolyards.org/tree-canopy-equity
    *   Brewing A Better Forest – connecting adopt tree model
*   **Data opportunities:**
    *   Analysis of lesson plans to quantify scheduled outdoor learning time.
    *   Chronological photo documentation and time-lapse storytelling.
    *   Crowd-sourced observations and stewardship activities.
    *   Defining and mapping property zones and student zones.
