1. The Cracker Soil Check

"Is the dirt dry like a cracker or moist like a sponge?"

Touch the top 2 inches of soil near the root ring. If it crumbles dry like a saltine cracker, time to call the water squad!

🪣

2. The Thirsty Sapling Sip

"Give 5 slow sips to the baby trees!"

Pour 5 gallons of water gently at the base of the tree so the water sinks straight into the deep taproots without washing away the soil.

🍩

3. The Mulch Donut Check

"Make a donut, not a volcano!"

Ensure the mulch looks like a flat donut with a 2-inch gap around the tree trunk. Never pile mulch against the trunk like a volcano!

🌱

4. Weed Invader Patrol

"Clear the boxing ring for our trees!"

Gently pull invasive crabgrass or Bermuda grass growing inside the mulch ring so the tree doesn't have to fight for soil nutrients.

🦋

5. Shade Habitat Spotter

"Who came to visit the cool branches today?"

Look quietly for 60 seconds. Spot a butterfly, honeybee, ladybug, or songbird resting in the cool canopy shade.
