🎯 Objective

Students learn to observe, monitor, and report on the health of trees over time, developing ongoing stewardship skills and ecological awareness.

📖 Overview

Studying a tree over time will allow students to observe seasonal changes and growth patterns from a specific tree species. In this activity, students/classes choose a tree (or a few trees) and spend the year recording observations and researching information.

Students/classes will record weekly observations to build a record that can be compared back to the baseline data. The tracking sheet will be sent to UTRGV to help organize the data.

📦 Materials

  • Measuring tape (for DBH)
  • Class observation log/journal
  • Pencil / clipboard
  • Optional: Soil measuring probe

🔬 Method

Step 1

Observations (Within the First Two Weeks of School)

Note: Record trees weekly (or at least biweekly).

🌱 Identification

Classes will use the school tree planting plan or research the type of tree using iNaturalist. Optional: Classes can choose their own nickname for their tree (be creative!).

📏 Tree Measurements

Diameter (DBH): Measure diameter at breast height (4.5 ft above ground). Optional: Have students hug the tree or use the string technique.

Height: Measure height (watch the Tree Height Video for triangulation & shadow techniques).

🩺 Tree Health Checkpoint

How happy and healthy does your tree seem? (Happy / Good, Medium / Fair, Sad / Bad).

  • Do the leaves appear bright green, dark green, or brown?
  • Do all the branches have leaves?
💧 Soil Moisture Check

Test moisture beneath the canopy and within the critical root zone (drip line), using fingers or a moisture probe.

Check: Are leaves/mulch covering the soil around the trunk of the tree?

🌿 Growth & Wildlife Notes

Note any flowers, fruits, seeds, or new green leaves. Important: If the tree appears unhealthy at any point, students report it to the teacher immediately.

Step 2

Reporting

After recording your observations — add them to the school bulletin board and the UTRGV dashboard. (Note: You can upload a picture or log entries directly into the dashboard below).
