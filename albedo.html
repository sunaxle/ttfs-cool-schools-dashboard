Why Does Tree Shade Make Such a Big Difference?

Dark asphalt absorbs over 90% of sunlight and bakes above 125°F. Tree leaves block that direct sunlight before it hits the ground and release cool moisture into the air, keeping shaded areas up to 35°F cooler!
