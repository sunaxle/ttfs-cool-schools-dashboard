Tree Stewardship Lesson

Connected lessons: Tree Journaling, Tree Measurement, Nature Journaling, Tree Detectives, Trees and Parasitism

Connected videos: Tree Height, Tree Measurement (Tree Age), Nature Journaling, Tree Care, Mindfulness in Nature, How Leaves help us Identify Plant Species, Plant ID with iNat

Objective: Students learn to observe, monitor, and report on the health of trees over time, developing ongoing stewardship skills and ecological awareness.

Overview: Studying a tree over time will allow students to observe seasonal changes and growth patterns from a specific tree species. In this activity, students/classes choose a tree (or a few trees) and spend the year recording observations and researching information. Students/classes will record weekly observations to build a record that can be compared back to the baseline data. The tracking sheet will be sent to UTRGV to help organize the data.

Materials: Measuring tape (for DBH), Class observation log/journal, Pencil/clipboard, Optional: soil measuring probe

Method Step 1 - Observations (within first two weeks of school): Note: record trees weekly (or at least biweekly)

  • Identification: classes will use the school tree planting plan or research the type of tree using iNaturalist. Optional: classes can choose their own nickname for their tree (be creative!)
  • Tree Measurements: Diameter (DBH) - Optional: have students hug the tree or use the string technique. Height (watch the Tree Height Video for technique)
  • Tree Health: Checkpoint: how happy and healthy does your tree seem (sad, medium, happy). Do the leaves appear bright green, dark green or brown? Do all the branches have leaves?
  • Soil moisture check: Test moisture beneath the canopy and within the critical root zone (drip line), using fingers or a moisture probe. leaves/mulch covering the soil around the trunk of the tree?
  • Growth & wildlife activity: Note any flowers, fruits, seeds, or new green leaves. If the tree appears unhealthy at any point, students report it to the teacher immediately.

Method Step 2 - Reporting: After recording your observations - add them to the school bulletin board and the UTRGV dashboard. Note: can upload a picture or directly into the dashboard

Tracking Table columns: Date, Tree (Nickname), Classroom, Species, Health Check (Good/Medium/Bad), DBH (inches), Height (feet), Moisture Check
