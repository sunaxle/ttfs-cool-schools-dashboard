Scientific Foundation & Validation

Harmonizing Campus Data with Global Science

Operationalizing the findings of Nature Communications (McDonald et al., 2026)

🎯
Closing the 1-km Gap
Global models average over 1 km grids, missing extreme local microclimates. Our 8-station campus arrays provide sub-meter empirical ground truth on school playgrounds.
☀️
Semi-Arid Efficiency
McDonald et al. proved Tree Cooling Efficiency (TCE) is highest in semi-arid and dense urban cores. Donna ISD and Mercedes ISD are prime real-world models.
🌡️
3.1x WBGT Shading
Solar radiation interception reduces Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT) 3.1x more than air temperature, dropping black globe heat by 25°F–40°F.
