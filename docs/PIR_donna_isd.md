**Subject:** Public Information Request — Anonymized Daily Attendance Records

Dear Donna ISD Records Custodian,

Pursuant to the **Texas Public Information Act** (Texas Government Code, Chapter 552), I am requesting the following public records:

### Records Requested

**Anonymized, campus-level daily attendance data** for all Donna ISD campuses for the following school years:
- 2021–2022
- 2022–2023
- 2023–2024
- 2024–2025

Specifically, for each campus and each school day, I am requesting:
1. **Campus name or campus ID number**
2. **Date**
3. **Total students enrolled (that day)**
4. **Total students present (that day)** or **daily attendance rate**

### Data Format
If available, I request this data in an electronic, machine-readable format (e.g., CSV, Excel, or delimited text file). If electronic format is not available, PDF or printed records are acceptable.

### Privacy Compliance
This request is limited to **aggregated, campus-level data only**. I am NOT requesting any student-level records, personally identifiable information (PII), or any data protected under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). Campus-level daily attendance totals are directory-level aggregate statistics and are not subject to FERPA restrictions.

### Purpose
This data will be used for a collaborative research project between the **University of Texas Rio Grande Valley (UTRGV)** and the **Texas Trees Foundation** investigating the relationship between extreme heat events and school attendance patterns in the Rio Grande Valley. All published findings will reference only aggregated data and will not identify individual students.

### Fee Waiver Request
I respectfully request a waiver of any fees associated with this request, as the data will be used for academic research conducted in partnership with a public university (UTRGV) and a nonprofit organization (Texas Trees Foundation) for the direct benefit of Donna ISD students. If a fee waiver is not possible, please notify me of estimated costs before proceeding.

### Response Timeline
Under the Texas Public Information Act, I understand that you must respond to this request within **10 business days** of receipt (Texas Government Code §552.301).

Thank you for your time and assistance. Please do not hesitate to contact me with any questions regarding this request.
