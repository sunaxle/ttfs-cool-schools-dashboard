## 2. Detailed Deliverable Progress Achieved (August 2026)

### Deliverable A: Environmental Baseline Data Summary ($6,357.00 Earned)
* **High-Fidelity Multi-Campus GIS Digitization:** Expanded boundary digitization and surface area partitioning across participating Donna ISD and Mercedes ISD campuses.
* **Surface Breakdown Fact Sheets:** Locked high-precision baseline area tables for key pilot campuses (including M. Rivas Primary: 885,925 sq ft total boundary; 47.8% open field, 15.6% rooftops, 11.1% parking/hardscape, 0.5% shaded structures, 7.8% unmapped canopy buffer).
* **Data Quality Assurance (QA/QC):** Conducted topological consistency verification using Turf.js geometric validation algorithms to prevent polygon self-intersection and coordinate drift.

### Deliverable B: Environmental Monitoring Documentation Package ($4,843.00 Earned)
* **Training Materials v0.5 Released:** Finalized the core teacher and administrator onboarding presentation slide deck (`docs/donna_isd_presentation_slides.md`), complete with regional climate data, heat-attendance correlation models, and hands-on outdoor field activities.
* **Technical Assistance (TA) Log Schema Deployed:** Formatted the formal TA logging schema (`docs/Technical_Assistance_Log_v0.5.md`) to capture district support requests, response SLAs, and pedagogical assistance tracking under Texas A&M Forest Service compliance standards.
* **Standard Operating Procedures (SOP v0.6):** Maintained and reviewed field measurement protocols for tree caliper (DBH), canopy radius, and infrared surface temperature verification.

### Deliverable C: Forecasting & Digital Dashboard Support Materials ($4,314.00 Earned)
* **Environmental Modeling Visuals Package:** Compiled standardized diurnal surface heat figures (morning 9:00 AM, midday 1:00 PM peak, and evening 5:00 PM retention) and longitudinal 5-year/10-year canopy growth projection tables (`docs/Modeling_Visuals_Package.md`).
* **Dashboard v2 Architectural Planning Memo:** Published the comprehensive v2 roadmap (`docs/Dashboard_v2_Planning_Memo.md`) addressing stakeholder feedback, PWA offline field caching, and modular integrations.
* **New Prototype Portal Extensions Built:**
  1. *User Access Strategy & RBAC (`user_access_strategy.html`):* Visual mockups for public, educator, and administrative access tiers.
  2. *Campus Tree Sponsorship Portal (`donors_data.js` / `crm.js`):* Direct ecological ROI calculations for community and alumni tree donors.
  3. *Child-Friendly Habitat Spotter (`biodiversity_analog_concept.html`):* Gamified elementary biodiversity tracking module with zero-PII data capture.
  4. *TEKS-Aligned Lesson Plans (`teks_lesson_plans.html`):* Downloadable curriculum modules for grades 3–5 science standards.
* **Codebase Hardening:** Standardized 49 static pages with strict IIFE scopes, semantic ARIA roles, preconnected CDN links, and lazy asset loading.

## 3. Compliance, Governance, and Data Privacy Statement

* **FERPA & COPPA Zero-PII Compliance:** All prototype interfaces, student tracking tools, and field observation logs are strictly aggregated at the campus level. Zero personally identifiable student data is captured or retained.
* **Vendor Portability & Handoff Mandate:** The entire dashboard architecture remains 100% static client-side HTML5/CSS3/ES6 with no proprietary backend lock-in, ensuring seamless handoff to Texas Trees Foundation servers.
* **UTRGV AI Compliance:** All data models, GIS calculations, and documentation reflect human-in-the-loop oversight and rigorous peer-reviewed methodology.
