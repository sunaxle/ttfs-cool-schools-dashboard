## Context: The Mercedes ISD Comparison

Mercedes ISD lost approximately **281 enrolled students** in the first 13 days of the 2025–2026 school year. Using the same basic allotment rate:

- 281 students × $34.22/day × 13 days = **$125,032 in lost funding** in just the first two weeks
- 281 students × $34.22/day × 180 days = **$1,731,418 in annualized lost funding**
- **Result:** The district was forced to consolidate two middle schools (Sgt. William G. Harrell → Sgt. Manuel Chacon)

## Methodology Notes

- **Basic Allotment:** $6,160 is the HB 3 basic allotment as of 2024–2025. This is the *foundation* of state funding but does not include Tier 2 allotments, weighted funding for special populations, or local property tax contributions. Actual per-pupil revenue is higher.
- **Daily Rate:** Calculated as $6,160 ÷ 180 instructional days = $34.22/day.
- **ADA Drop Scenarios:** Calculated as (enrollment × drop percentage × daily rate). These represent state basic allotment losses only.
- **Tree Cost Estimates:** Based on typical municipal urban forestry program costs including procurement, planting, irrigation installation, and 3-year establishment maintenance. Actual costs vary by species, site conditions, and scale of planting.

> [!WARNING]
> These calculations use only the **basic allotment** ($6,160). Donna ISD's actual per-pupil revenue is significantly higher when weighted allotments and local funding are included. The true cost of absenteeism is therefore substantially greater than what is shown here. These figures represent a conservative floor.
