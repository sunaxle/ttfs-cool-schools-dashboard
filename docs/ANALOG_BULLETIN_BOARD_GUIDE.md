# 📌 The Campus Living Tree Bulletin Board
## 1-Page Setup & Monthly Photo-Reporting Guide

> **Audience:** Campus Principals, Science Leads, Art Teachers, & Student Council  
> **Purpose:** A collective, analog space for elementary students to share tree photos, nature drawings, and outdoor stories—with a **60-second monthly photo submission** that updates the Cool Schools Dashboard.  
> **Privacy Mandate:** 100% FERPA/COPPA Compliant (Zero student PII or face photos required).  

---

### Step 1: Set Up Your Physical Board (15 Minutes)

Find a high-traffic corkboard in your main hallway, library, or cafeteria. Divide it into **3 Creative Zones**:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  🌳 OUR CAMPUS LIVING TREE & SHADE BOARD                                          |
+--------------------------+----------------------------+---------------------------+
|  📸 ZONE 1:              |  🎨 ZONE 2:                |  ✍️ ZONE 3:               |
|  Tree Photos & Growth    |  Student Art & Rubbings    |  Micro-Stories & Spotting |
|                          |                            |                           |
|  • Photos of school trees|  • Colored leaf rubbings   |  • "Why I love our tree"  |
|  • Class watering teams  |  • Tree canopy sketches    |  • "Today I saw a bird/bug|
|  • Tree tag # measurements| • Seasonal bark drawings  |    sitting in the shade!" |
+--------------------------+----------------------------+---------------------------+
|  🏆 CAMPUS STEWARDSHIP BADGE METER: [ 🌱 Level 1 ] -> [ 🌿 Level 2 ] -> [ 🌳 Level 3 ] |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

### Step 2: The 60-Second Monthly Reporting Routine

On the **last Friday of every month**, the designated Admin or Science Lead completes reporting in 3 simple steps:

1. **Snap One Photo:** Stand 5 feet back and take one clear, well-lit photo of the entire bulletin board on your phone.
2. **Scan & Upload:** Scan your school's unique Cool Schools QR code (or visit `coolschools.utrgv.edu/upload`) and attach the photo.
3. **Add a 1-Sentence Caption:** *(e.g., "October Fall Leaf Collection & 4th Grade Cedar Elm Measurement Day at M. Rivas Primary")*.

### 🔒 Zero-PII Privacy Checklist
- [x] Use first names only (or class grade: *"4th Grade Room 102"*) on drawings.
- [x] Focus photos on the trees, leaves, bark rubbings, and the board itself.
- [x] No sensitive student records or individual faces required for submission.
