## 4. Standardized Monthly Review Workflow
To keep reviews simple and predictable for both UTRGV and Texas Trees Foundation staff throughout 2027:
1. **Live Staging Preview (Continuous):** All dashboard updates are published to the live GitHub Pages staging environment (`https://sunaxle.github.io/ttfs-cool-schools-dashboard/`) so TTFS staff can inspect changes in any browser with zero software installation.
2. **Monthly Deliverable Packet (End of Month):** UTRGV submits the monthly 1-page *Description of Deliverables*, *Monthly Progress Report*, and *Milestone Invoice* to TTFS Operations and Grants Accounting.
3. **District Feedback Loop (Ongoing):** As Donna ISD and Mercedes ISD teachers share feedback on the TEKS lessons and field journals, TTFS relays those notes to UTRGV for rapid integration during the Q1/Q2 2027 training cycles.
