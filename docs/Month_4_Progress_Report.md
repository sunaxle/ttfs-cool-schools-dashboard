## 2. Detailed Deliverable Progress Achieved (September 2026)

### Deliverable A: Environmental Baseline Data Summary
**September Milestone Earned: $3,390.00** *(Cumulative: $47,685.00 / 87.2% of Deliverable A Allocation)*

In accordance with Exhibit D milestones ("Figure templates and caption conventions" and "Baseline refinements"), the UTRGV team executed the following baseline advancements:

1. **Publication-Grade Cartographic & Figure Conventions:**
   - Standardized uniform cartographic styling rules across all 13 study sites in Donna ISD and Mercedes ISD.
   - Established fixed thermal infrared palettes ($80^\circ\text{F}$ dark forest green to $140^\circ\text{F}$ intense crimson) and Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) color ramps ($0.0$ to $0.85$).
   - Standardized typography, scale bars, true north indicators, and bilingual (English/Spanish) captioning formats (`docs/Modeling_Visuals_Package.md`) to support both university research dissemination and district community outreach.

2. **Regional 30% Canopy Baseline Cross-Examination & Heuristic Analysis:**
   - Completed an in-depth ecological review of the municipal planning constant ($1,314\text{ sq ft}$ mature canopy per tree, representing a $40.9\text{ ft}$ crown diameter) utilized in Studio Outside / Heffner Design Team landscape architectural calculations (`docs/CANOPY_CONSTANT_1314_ANALYSIS.md`).
   - Evaluated species-specific variances across the South Texas native planting palette:
     - **Southern Live Oak (*Quercus virginiana*):** $1,963 – 4,418\text{ sq ft}$ mature spread ($+49\%\text{ to }+236\%$ above heuristic).
     - **Cedar Elm (*Ulmus crassifolia*) & Post Oak (*Quercus stellata*):** $962 – 1,963\text{ sq ft}$ (closely aligned with heuristic).
     - **Velvet Ash (*Fraxinus velutina*):** $707 – 1,257\text{ sq ft}$ ($-46\%\text{ to }-4\%$).
     - **Small Ornamentals & Palms (*Lagerstroemia indica*, *Chilopsis linearis*, *Sabal mexicana*):** $79 – 314\text{ sq ft}$ ($-76\%\text{ to }-94\%$ shade deficit vs. heuristic).
   - Formulated the **"Canopy Deficit Risk"** model for school district grounds committees: demonstrating that substituting large shade overstory trees with ornamental or palm species can result in up to a 60% deficit in delivered thermal cooling, even if numerical tree planting counts are met.

3. **Multi-Campus Baseline Inventory & Deficit Synthesis:**
   - Synthesized the complete master baseline dataset across 13 campus sites (14 schools) covering **8,449,178 sq ft (194.0 acres)** of total studied schoolyard land and **3,896,912 sq ft (89.5 acres)** of non-programmed green space (`docs/REGIONAL_CANOPY_30_PERCENT_STUDY_MASTER.md`).
   - Reconciled existing baseline canopy (**309,401 sq ft**, or **7.9%** of available green space) against the 30% canopy target (**1,169,075 sq ft**), locking the net regional planting requirement at **+661 mature shade trees** across Donna ISD (+451 trees needed across 7 sites) and Mercedes ISD (+210 net trees needed across 6 sites).

4. **Multi-Agent Research Provenance & Lineage Integration:**
   - Consolidated research lineage ledgers (`docs/research_provenance_and_lineage.md`) connecting Texas Academic Performance Reports (TAPR) and PEIMS attendance metrics with National Weather Service (NWS) Brownsville extreme heat data.
   - Formalized econometric and cognitive regression models (Park et al., 2020: 1% cognitive decline per $1^\circ\text{F}$ rise above baseline; TEA Texas Education Code §25.081 low-attendance waiver thresholds at 10% daily attendance drops).

### Deliverable C: Forecasting & Digital Dashboard Support Materials
**September Milestone Earned: $3,632.00** *(Cumulative: $23,363.00 / 41.9% of Deliverable C Allocation)*

In accordance with Exhibit D milestones ("Dashboard v2 early components" and "Dashboard usability pass; document limits and data caveats"), the team accomplished the following software, data modeling, and usability milestones:

1. **Dashboard v2 Early Component Architecture & Performance Tuning:**
   - Executed frontend performance enhancements across all 49 modular HTML/CSS/JS prototype views.
   - Enforced strict IIFE scoping and zero inline scripts/styles across all modules to eliminate global namespace collisions and ensure clean client-side security.
   - Configured preconnect headers for external CDN dependencies (D3.js, Chart.js, Three.js, ArcGIS API for JavaScript) and applied native browser `loading="lazy"` attributes across all photographic and raster visual assets.
   - Structured the Progressive Web App (PWA) Service Worker caching manifest to enable zero-connectivity field data viewing on outdoor schoolyard tablets.

2. **Dashboard Usability Pass & Data Caveats Documentation:**
   - Authored formal data lineage and usability caveats documentation for district leadership and campus facility managers (`docs/Dashboard_v2_Planning_Memo.md` and `docs/research_provenance_and_lineage.md`).
   - Clearly documented key modeling assumptions and operational limitations:
     - **PEIMS Attendance Granularity Caveat:** Public TEA/PEIMS attendance records are published annually at the campus level; daily/weekly correlation studies require formal district Public Information Requests (PIRs) currently underway with Donna ISD and Mercedes ISD.
     - **Diurnal Surface Temperature Modeling Assumptions:** Thermal infrared curves model three distinct solar benchmarks: 9:00 AM morning baseline ($84^\circ\text{F}$ air / $92^\circ\text{F}$ turf), 1:00 PM solar noon peak ($96^\circ\text{F}$ air / $138^\circ\text{F}$ unshaded asphalt vs. $87^\circ\text{F}$ tree shade / $94^\circ\text{F}$ shaded turf), and 5:00 PM thermal retention ($93^\circ\text{F}$ air / $126^\circ\text{F}$ hardscape).
     - **Geometric QA/QC Tolerances:** Spatial boundary calculations utilize Turf.js automated topological validation to ensure zero unclosed polygons or coordinate drift.

3. **Core Interactive Feature Extensions & Prototype Hardening:**
   - **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) System (`user_access_strategy.html`):** Formalized 3-tier user clearance pathways (Public/Student zero-login explorer, Educator password-gated TEKS repository, and Administrator/TTFS spatial data console).
   - **Child-Friendly Habitat Spotter (`biodiversity_analog_concept.html`):** Deployed gamified wildlife and pollinator logging interfaces designed specifically for elementary student usability with zero-PII data capture.
   - **Living Tree Legacy & 10-Year Time Machine (`tree_diary.html`, `time_machine.html`):** Finalized interactive decade shade scrubber modeling longitudinal tree growth, cumulative stormwater interception, and student cohort naming history over the 10-year project lifecycle.
   - **TEKS-Aligned Lesson Plans Portal (`teks_lesson_plans.html`):** Packaged downloadable 45-minute science lesson modules (TEKS 3.9A, 4.9A, 5.9A) connecting tree crown measurement and microclimate temperature logging to elementary state standards.

## 3. Governance, Privacy, and Compliance Statement

The UTRGV evaluation team certifies full adherence to the project's non-negotiable governance principles:

1. **Privacy-First Architecture (COPPA & FERPA Compliance):**
   - No Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is captured, stored, or transmitted anywhere within the digital dashboard, student activity modules, or field forms.
   - Student observations (Tree Diaries, Habitat Spotting) are strictly anonymized and aggregated at the campus cohort level.

2. **The Handoff Mandate & Zero Vendor Lock-In:**
   - The entire dashboard prototype is engineered as a 100% portable, static client-side web application (HTML5, CSS3, ES6 JavaScript) requiring no proprietary server backends, database licenses, or runtime hosting dependencies.
   - The entire codebase is fully deliverable and deployable to standard Texas Trees Foundation web hosting environments.

3. **UTRGV AI Compliance (Effective Dec 1, 2025):**
   - All AI-assisted research and computational modeling have been conducted using approved enterprise platforms with strict human-in-the-loop validation by PI Dr. Alexis Racelis and the UTRGV research team. Zero confidential university or student records were uploaded or processed.

4. **Human-Analog UI Design Philosophy:**
   - Interfaces maintain a clean, productive, and tactile aesthetic favoring functional split-screen layouts, high-contrast readable type, and physical flyer/guide analogs over cluttered or automated dashboard templates.
