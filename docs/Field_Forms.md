## Biodiversity Tracking (iNaturalist Integration)
*Note: This section relies on crowd-sourced citizen science to avoid collecting student PII.*

**Instructions for Field Collection:**
1. Download and open the **iNaturalist app** on your mobile device or tablet.
2. Ensure your device's GPS / Location Services are turned **ON** so observations are accurately geotagged to the campus.
3. Snap a clear photo of the wildlife (birds, insects, mammals, or native plants) utilizing the tree or campus area.
4. Upload the observation to iNaturalist. You do not need to know the exact species; the app's AI and the iNaturalist community will help identify it.
5. **Dashboard Integration:** No manual data entry on this sheet is required for wildlife! The Cool Schools dashboard is configured to automatically scrape and pull any verified iNaturalist observations that are tagged within the geographic boundaries of this campus.
