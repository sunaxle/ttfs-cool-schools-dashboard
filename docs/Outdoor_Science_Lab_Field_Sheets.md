### 👥 Team Cooperative Learning Roles
*Assign one role badge to each student on your 5-member field team:*
* `[ ]` **1. Thermal Scout:** Operates the handheld infrared laser thermometer and reads temperatures.
* `[ ]` **2. Caliper Tech:** Measures trunk circumference/DBH using the flexible caliper tape.
* `[ ]` **3. Canopy Pacer:** Counts giant walking steps from trunk to outer shade drip-line edge.
* `[ ]` **4. Field Scribe:** Holds clipboard and records all numbers neatly on this worksheet.
* `[ ]` **5. Wildlife Spotter:** Observes and tallies birds, insects, and pollinators around the tree.

---

### 🛡️ Field Safety & Tool Golden Rules
1. **Laser Safety:** Never point the infrared thermometer's red laser at anyone's eyes, face, or reflective glass. Point only at the ground or tree trunk from 12–24 inches away.
2. **Thermal Touch Rule:** **DO NOT TOUCH** sunny blacktop or metal playground equipment with bare skin ($160^\circ\text{F}+$ contact burn hazard).
3. **Buddy System:** Stay within your designated campus station area with your team at all times.
