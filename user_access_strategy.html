Platform Access & Governance Strategy

This document outlines the proposed Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) architecture to ensure compliance with FERPA, COPPA, and university policies while maximizing public engagement.

Access Hierarchy Diagram

University Admins / Researchers
(Full System Access)
➔
Educators / Teachers
(Curriculum & Tracking Access)
➔
Students / Public
(Anonymized Exploratory Access)

Level 1: System Administrators & Researchers

High Clearance

Full unhindered access to raw data, system logs, sensor telemetry, and platform configurations. Required for grant reporting and backend maintenance.

Accessible Modules:

✔️ Raw Data Pipelines
✔️ Maintenance Trackers
✔️ Economic ROI / Budgeting
✔️ i-Tree Eco Ledger
✔️ PII-Visible Student Tracking
✔️ SOP Protocols & Uploads

Level 2: Principals & Educators

Secure Gateway

Authenticated portal for school staff. Provides access to lesson plans, student engagement metrics, and class-level analytics without exposing backend university tools.

Accessible Modules:

✔️ TEKS Lesson Plans Portal
✔️ Anonymized Student Tracking
✔️ Pre/Post Survey Gateways
✔️ Canopy Over Time
✔️ Dashboard Overview
❌ Raw Sensor Configurations

Level 3: Students, Parents & General Public

Open Access (Anonymized)

COPPA/FERPA compliant public tier. No PII is collected or displayed. Focuses entirely on educational games, simulations, and community engagement features.

Accessible Modules:

✔️ Educational Biodiversity Portal
✔️ Tree Sponsorship Portal (Alumni)
✔️ 3D Shade Models
✔️ Weather & Temp Simulations
❌ TEKS Lesson Plans (Keys/Answers)
❌ Individual Student Tracking Data
