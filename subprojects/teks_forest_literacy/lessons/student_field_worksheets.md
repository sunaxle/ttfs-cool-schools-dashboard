## Worksheet 2: Tree Geometry & Shadow Area Calculator (Grades 3–5)

```text
================================================================================
                    CAMPUS MICROFOREST FIELD INVESTIGATION
                    WORKSHEET 2: TREE GEOMETRY & COOLING SHADE AREA
================================================================================
Student Name: ____________________________ Date: ______________________________
Partner Name: ____________________________ Time of Day: ________________________

[ STEP 1: CALIBRATE YOUR PERSONAL STEP PACE ]
1. Walk 10 normal steps along a 10-meter tape measure.
2. Distance walked: _________ meters.
3. My average pace = (Distance ÷ 10) = _________ meters per step.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ STEP 2: MEASURE THE CANOPY SHADOW RADIUS ]
Stand at the center of the tree trunk and pace outward to the edge of the shadow:
- North Shadow Radius (r1): _____ steps x (My pace: _____) = ________ meters
- South Shadow Radius (r2): _____ steps x (My pace: _____) = ________ meters
- East Shadow Radius  (r3): _____ steps x (My pace: _____) = ________ meters
- West Shadow Radius  (r4): _____ steps x (My pace: _____) = ________ meters

Average Radius (r_avg) = (r1 + r2 + r3 + r4) ÷ 4 = ________ meters.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ STEP 3: CALCULATE THE COOLING GROUND AREA ]
Formula for Area of a Circle: Area = π × (r_avg)²   (Use π ≈ 3.14)

Calculation:
   Area = 3.14 × (_________ meters)²
   Area = 3.14 × (_________ )
   Area = ________________ square meters of cool shade!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ STEP 4: INFRARED SURFACE TEMPERATURE AUDIT ]
Measure surface temperatures with your infrared thermometer gun:
- Temperature of Blacktop Asphalt in full sun:  ___________ °F
- Temperature of Grass beneath Tree Shade:     ___________ °F
- COOLING DIFFERENCE (Asphalt Temp - Shade Temp): ___________ °F Cooler!
```

## Worksheet 3: Clinometer Trigonometry & Tree Height (Grades 6–8)

```text
================================================================================
                    CAMPUS MICROFOREST FIELD INVESTIGATION
                    WORKSHEET 3: FORESTRY TRIGONOMETRY & TREE HEIGHT
================================================================================
Student Name: ____________________________ Date: ______________________________
Class Period: ____________________________ Tree Species: _______________________

[ STEP 1: RIGHT TRIANGLE TRIGONOMETRY FORMULA ]
             
             /|  <-- Top of Tree (Angle sighted: θ)
            / |
           /  | Tree Height (H) = (Distance × tan θ) + Eye Height
          /   |
         /θ___|
     Eye |    |
   Height|____| Base of Tree
         |<--- Baseline Distance (D) --->|

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ STEP 2: FIELD MEASUREMENTS ]
1. Baseline Distance (D) from tree base:  _______________ meters (Recommended: 15m)
2. Sighted Clinometer Zenith Angle (θ):  _______________ degrees
3. Eye-Level Height of Sighter (h_eye):   _______________ meters

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ STEP 3: COMPUTATION TABLE ]

| Angle (θ) | tan(θ) Value | Distance (D) | D × tan(θ) | Eye Height (h_eye) | Total Tree Height (H) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| _____ ° | __________ | _____ m | _____ m | + _____ m | = ___________ meters |

*Trig Reference Table:*
- tan(30°) = 0.577  |  tan(35°) = 0.700  |  tan(40°) = 0.839  |  tan(45°) = 1.000
- tan(50°) = 1.192  |  tan(55°) = 1.428  |  tan(60°) = 1.732  |  tan(65°) = 2.145

Analysis Question: If this tree grows at an average rate of 0.4 meters per year,
what will its projected height be when you graduate from high school?
Projected Height = Current Height (_____) + (Years: _____ × 0.4m) = _______ meters.
```

## Worksheet 4: Allometric Biomass & Whole-Forest Carbon Vault (Grades 9–12 / APES)

```text
================================================================================
                    CAMPUS MICROFOREST FIELD INVESTIGATION
                    WORKSHEET 4: ALLOMETRIC BIOMASS & CARBON STORAGE
================================================================================
Student Researcher: ______________________ Date: ______________________________
Course / Section:   ______________________ Campus Zone: ________________________

[ ALLOMETRIC POWER-LAW MODEL ]
Dry Aboveground Biomass (M_kg) = exp( β0 + β1 × ln(DBH_cm) )
Carbon Mass (C_kg) = 0.50 × M_kg
Total CO2 Equivalent (CO2e_kg) = C_kg × (44 / 12) ≈ C_kg × 3.67

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ FIELD MEASUREMENT TALLY MATRIX ]

Measure 4 tagged campus trees at Breast Height (4.5 ft / 1.37 m above ground):

| Tree # | Species Common Name | Trunk DBH (cm) | Dry Biomass (kg) | Carbon Stored (kg C) | Total CO2e Sequestered (kg) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 01 | Honey Mesquite       | ________ cm    | ________ kg      | ________ kg C        | ________ kg CO2e            |
| 02 | Texas Ebony         | ________ cm    | ________ kg      | ________ kg C        | ________ kg CO2e            |
| 03 | Cedar Elm           | ________ cm    | ________ kg      | ________ kg C        | ________ kg CO2e            |
| 04 | Montezuma Cypress   | ________ cm    | ________ kg      | ________ kg C        | ________ kg CO2e            |

*Species Allometric Coefficients:*
- Honey Mesquite (*Prosopis glandulosa*): ln(M) = -2.13 + 2.45 × ln(DBH)
- Texas Ebony (*Ebenopsis ebano*):       ln(M) = -1.98 + 2.38 × ln(DBH)
- Cedar Elm (*Ulmus crassifolia*):        ln(M) = -2.25 + 2.48 × ln(DBH)
- Montezuma Cypress (*Taxodium muc.*):   ln(M) = -2.05 + 2.52 × ln(DBH)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ MONETIZED ECOSYSTEM SERVICES VALUATION ]
Sum of Campus Sample CO2e = _____________ kg CO2e = _____________ Metric Tons CO2e
At a social cost of carbon of $51 / metric ton, what is the monetized value of
this microforest carbon storage?
Monetized Carbon Asset Value = (_____ Metric Tons) × $51 = $________________ USD.
```

## Worksheet 5: DIY Olla Pot Sub-Surface Irrigation Log (Parent / Community)

```text
================================================================================
                    COMMUNITY AGROECOLOGY WORKSHOP
                    WORKSHEET 5: OLLA POT IRRIGATION & SOIL CARE
================================================================================
Family / Household Name: ________________________________ Date: _______________
Neighborhood / City:     ________________________________ Tree Name: ___________

[ STEP 1: OLLA ASSEMBLY CHECKLIST ]
[  ] 1. Clean unglazed 10-inch terracotta pot and matching flat saucer.
[  ] 2. Apply 100% waterproof silicone sealant to bottom drainage hole. Let dry 24h.
[  ] 3. Test for leaks by filling with 1 cup of water on a dry paper towel.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ STEP 2: BURYING & INSTALLATION ]
[  ] 1. Dig hole 12 inches away from young tree trunk, 10 inches deep.
[  ] 2. Place terracotta pot in hole; top rim should sit 1 inch above soil surface.
[  ] 3. Backfill gently with native soil and pack lightly.
[  ] 4. Fill olla with water and place inverted terracotta saucer on top as a lid.
[  ] 5. Apply 3 inches of wood-chip mulch around tree (keep mulch 2 inches off bark).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ WEEKLY WATERING LOG ]

| Week | Date Checked | Olla Water Level (Full / Half / Empty) | Refilled (Yes/No) | Soil Touch Test (Cracker / Cookie Crumb) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |

| 02 | ____ / ____ | ______________________________________ | _________________ | ________________________________________ |
```
