    📜 Academic Provenance & Attribution Archive

    The Comprehensive TEKS Forest Literacy Learning Architecture is built upon decades of peer-reviewed research, state educational standards, and indigenous ethnobotanical scholarship:

    1. Project Learning Tree (PLT) & Sustainable Forestry Initiative

    All learning nodes are indexed against the national Forest Literacy Framework (Themes 1–4: What is a Forest, Why Forests Matter, How We Sustain Forests, and What is Our Responsibility).

    2. Texas A&M Forest Service (TFS)

    Dendrology identification keys and native South Texas thornscrub allometric biomass equations are adapted from the TFS Trees of Texas research database.

    3. Texas Trees Foundation (TTF)

    Cool Schools microforest planting protocols, 10-year growth projections, and urban heat island mitigation standards.

    4. USDA Forest Service & Davey Tree Expert Co. (i-Tree Suite)

    Peer-reviewed mathematical algorithms for carbon sequestration, stormwater interception, and air pollution filtration calculations.

    5. Indigenous Ethnobotanical Heritage of South Texas

    Traditional ecological knowledge of the Coahuiltecan, Karankawa, and Tamaulipan peoples regarding the Honey Mesquite (Pechita), Prickly Pear, and Rio Grande riverway ecosystems.
